## What each told us (the shape, not just pass/fail)
1. **SEQUENTIAL inverted (-0.045):** serial objectives = serial GATES = serial misclassification by
   priors. Each gate strips what the next can't recover -> monotone decay = the objectives INTERACT
   and gating destroys the interaction. => objectives must be taken IN CONJUNCTION, not as gates.
2. **CONJUNCTION drop-then-FLAT (-0.027):** joint pricing ARRESTED the serial compounding (k2->k3
   flat, CI straddles 0, vs sequential's continued -0.013) -> PREVENTION is real. But still net
   negative -> prevention NECESSARY-NOT-SUFFICIENT. Adding finer objectives onto ONE substrate (even
   conjoined) dilutes the abstract readout. => don't ACCUMULATE; the abstract tier wants its coarse
   objective ALONE (omission, confirmed a 3rd way; k1 is the optimum in every architecture).
3. **SUPERSESSION flipped POSITIVE (+0.017, CI>0):** keep the abstract tier CLEAN (separate model)
   and add the finer objective as a SEPARATE correction store that OVERRIDES the abstract tier's
   cross-class errors, precondition-conditional. First positive direction in the arc. Sub the +0.02
   gate -> verdict CORRECTION_NULL on selectivity-magnitude, but the DISCIPLINE gate PASSED hard.

## The decisive finding: the discipline IS the mechanism (G_disc +0.145)
- Blanket override (always use the better order-specialist) = **0.517, WORSE than doing nothing.**
- Precondition-conditional override (fire only when the abstract tier flags its own class/order
  inconsistency, 12.7% of cases) = **0.662.**
- **+0.145 gap. "Use the better model" destroys abstraction; "override only under matching
  preconditions" builds it.** = the rapide_store / RMD-SRC supersession principle, confirmed.
- WHY (generalizable): a finer specialist is better at its level but WORSE at respecting the coarse
  structure when it errs (its mistakes cross coarse boundaries). So a correction store must supersede
  SELECTIVELY — capture the specialist's fixes only where the abstract tier flags confusion, never
  wholesale.
